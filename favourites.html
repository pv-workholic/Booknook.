THE BOOKS THAT STAYED

My Favourites

Not necessarily the best books ever written. Just the ones that refused to leave.
Some books entertain us for a few days. Some stay on our shelves. And a rare few quietly move into our thoughts, changing the way we see stories, people, and sometimes even ourselves. These are the books I return to in conversations, recommend without hesitation, and think about long after the final page. If Book Nook is a map of my reading life, this shelf is the heart of it.
PERSONAL COLLECTION

A Shelf Built From Favourite Memories

This isn't a list of the greatest books ever written. It's a collection of stories that arrived at the right time, introduced unforgettable characters, and somehow stayed longer than expected.

Some made me laugh. Some made me cry. Some made me stare at a wall after finishing them. And a few quietly became part of who I am as a reader.

The books on this shelf aren't perfect. They're personal.
