ARCHIVE 03

Evidence Room

Proof that books leave traces behind.
Most books end when the final page does.

Some don't.

They leave fragments behind. A sentence. A feeling. A thought that returns weeks later for no apparent reason.

This is a collection of those fragments. The evidence that certain books were here.

The Echoes

Most stories end.

The strange ones don't.

They echo. Through sentences. Through memories. Through random afternoons years later.

Long after the cover is closed. Long after the page number is forgotten.

This room is full of echoes.
