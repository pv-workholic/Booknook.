OUR PERFECT STORM
Author: Carley Fortune • Pages: ~336
★★★★☆
The Premise

Two people reconnect during what was meant to be a temporary trip, but nothing about it stays temporary for long. What begins as a chance encounter slowly deepens into something emotionally complicated—built on unfinished conversations, lingering memories, and feelings that were never fully resolved.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What Makes It Worth Picking Up

A soft, atmospheric romance shaped by nostalgia, timing, and the quiet question of “what if.” It leans heavily into mood—summer air, quiet tension, and emotional pauses that say more than dialogue ever could. It’s less about falling in love, and more about returning to a version of yourself you thought you had already outgrown.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
My Reading Experience

This book felt like being caught in an emotional tide 🌊😭

It starts gently—almost comforting—and then slowly you realize you’re deeply attached to people making very questionable emotional decisions 🫠💔

I kept thinking: “just communicate properly,” while also rooting for them to figure it out anyway 💭😭
