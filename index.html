About Me

A reader trying to become a writer.

Hey There, I'm Pranjal.

I just closed the chapter on my school years and am officially dedicating my time to the two things I love most: reading books and learning how to write them.

My obsession with literature spans across all genres. If a book can make me think, cry, laugh, or question reality, it belongs on my shelf.

I started this site to document my reading journey, deconstruct what makes a story truly unforgettable, and connect with fellow book lovers.

Thank you for stopping by.

Happy Reading 🥰

— Pranjal
