A reader trying to become a writer.

Some stories stay behind in highlighted passages, rainy evenings, coffee stains, and fictional characters that continue living rent-free in my mind long after the final page. Book Nook is where I collect those stories, those thoughts, and the occasional emotional damage caused by fictional people.

A Reader's Introduction

About Me

Hey there,
I'm Pranjal.

I recently closed the chapter on my school years and am officially dedicating my time to two things I love most: reading books and learning how to write them.

My obsession with literature spans across genres. If a book can make me think, cry, laugh, question reality, or keep me awake until 3 AM, it has a place on my shelf.

I started Book Nook as a literary journal — a place to document my reading journey, collect unforgettable quotes, leave behind midnight annotations, and celebrate stories that linger long after the final page.

Thank you for stopping by.
Happy Reading ♥️

— Pranjal
