A NOTE LEFT BETWEEN THE PAGES

Dear Reader,

If you've wandered this far through the shelves, thank you for spending a little time in my corner of the literary world.

Some stories stay with us for a weekend. Some stay for a lifetime.

These books earned their place here because they made readers laugh, cry, wonder, and occasionally stare at the wall after turning the final page.

But this shelf is far from complete. More books are waiting to be discovered. More reviews are waiting to be written. And more fictional characters are preparing to take over my thoughts.

Until then, keep your bookmark nearby. There are many more stories on their way.

— Pranjal
