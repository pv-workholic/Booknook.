The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo is one of those rare books that truly lives up to the hype.Evelyn is unforgettable — ambitious, flawed, courageous, and deeply human. With every chapter, another layer unfolds, making it impossible to look away. What begins as a story about fame slowly transforms into something far more intimate — a reflection on identity, love, and the quiet cost of ambition. Long after the final page, it stays with you.
