THE HOUSEMAID
Author: Freida McFadden • Pages: ~320
★★★★☆
The Premise

Millie is given a fresh start when she becomes a live-in housemaid for the wealthy Winchester family. The job seems perfect at first—beautiful house, steady pay, and a chance to leave her past behind. But the longer she stays, the more she senses that something about the household isn’t right.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What Makes It Worth Picking Up

A fast-paced psychological thriller built on secrets, manipulation, and shifting power dynamics. The short chapters and sharp pacing make it incredibly addictive—you keep reading “just one more chapter” until it’s suddenly 2 AM.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
My Reading Experience

This was one of those books where I kept saying “last chapter” and absolutely lying to myself 😭📖

Every time I thought I understood what was happening, the story quietly shifted and made me question everything again 🫠

It pulls you into its rhythm so quickly that you don’t even notice how fast you’re flying through it.
