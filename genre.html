BEFORE YOU WANDER

A Letter To Fellow Readers

Dear Reader,

Every bookshelf tells a different story. Some readers love dragons and destiny. Some prefer mysteries that keep them awake until 2 AM. Others want characters who feel like old friends.

This page isn't a list of the "best" books. It's a literary map. A collection of shelves waiting to be explored.

Wander wherever your curiosity takes you. You might discover your next favourite book on a shelf you never expected to visit.
— Pranjal 📖

Classics

Stories that survived generations, outlived trends, and still manage to feel surprisingly modern.

Pride and Prejudice

Jane Austen
⚠ Reader Advisory

Contains excessive yearning, social embarrassment, dramatic misunderstandings, and one man who desperately needed better communication skills.

Why Read It

Sharp wit, unforgettable characters, and one of literature's greatest romances.

Perfect For

Readers who love character-driven stories, slow-burn romance, and clever dialogue.

Book Nook Verdict: Like drinking tea with very dramatic people and somehow enjoying every second of it.
Timeless
Slow Burn
Re-read Worthy
Iconic Heroine

Jane Eyre

Charlotte Brontë
⚠ Travel Notice

Includes mysterious mansions, questionable life choices, and enough gothic atmosphere to power an entire season of Netflix.

Why Read It

A powerful story about independence, self-respect, love, and resilience.

Perfect For

Readers who enjoy strong protagonists, gothic settings, and emotional depth.

Book Nook Verdict: Dark, beautiful, and impossible to forget.
